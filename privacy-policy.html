Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026
This Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal data in connection with this website, in line with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019.

1. Who we are

Haji Mukhtar Properties & Petroleum Ltd ("we", "us", "our") is a company incorporated in Kenya (Reg. No. PVT-5JUZ65KA), with offices in Tudor, Mombasa. For the purposes of the Data Protection Act, 2019, we act as the data controller for personal data collected through this website. You can reach us at investors@hajimukhtar.com.

2. What we collect

We collect only the information you choose to provide, and limited technical information necessary to operate the site:

  • Enquiry details you submit through our contact form — your name, email address, organisation, area of interest and any message you include.
  • Technical data generated automatically when you visit, such as IP address and browser information, through our hosting and content-delivery providers' standard server logs.

We do not knowingly collect special categories of personal data, and we do not use advertising or behavioural-tracking cookies on this site.

3. How and why we use it

We use your information to respond to your enquiry, to assess and discuss potential investment, partnership or commercial relationships, and to keep records of our communications. Our lawful bases for processing are your consent (which you give by submitting the form) and our legitimate interest in responding to and managing enquiries.

4. Service providers and sharing

We use trusted third parties to operate the site and process enquiries, including our website host, our form-handling provider and our web-font provider. These providers process data on our behalf or as part of delivering their service. We do not sell your personal data. We may disclose information where required by law or to protect our legal rights.

5. International transfers

Some of our service providers operate outside Kenya, which means your information may be processed in other countries. Where this happens, we take reasonable steps to ensure your information continues to be protected to a standard consistent with the Data Protection Act, 2019.

6. Retention

We keep personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes described above, or as required to meet legal or record-keeping obligations, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

7. Your rights

Subject to the Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to be informed about how your data is used, and to request access to, correction, deletion or restriction of your personal data, and to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at investors@hajimukhtar.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) in Kenya.

8. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against loss, misuse and unauthorised access. No method of transmission over the internet is entirely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or your personal data can be sent to investors@hajimukhtar.com.
