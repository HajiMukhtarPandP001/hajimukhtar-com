A WORD FROM OUR FOUNDER

Building for the long term.

When I founded Haji Mukhtar Properties & Petroleum, I set out to build something the Kenyan coast could depend on — not quick wins, but real assets that hold their value and serve the people around them.

Today we develop homes along the shoreline and move the fuel that keeps the region working. Two very different businesses, yet one discipline runs through both: build well, deliver what we promise, and grow value that is secured against something tangible.

To the partners and investors who choose to build with us — thank you. There is a great deal still to do on this coast, and we intend to do it properly, and for the long term.

Abdi Mukhtar Mohamed

THE COMPANY

One company, two engines of value.

Haji Mukhtar Properties & Petroleum Ltd pairs the long-term appreciation of coastal real estate with the steady cash flow of downstream fuel — two divisions that balance and reinforce each other.

The company is Kenyan-incorporated and Mombasa-based, led by founder Abdi Mukhtar Mohamed, and built for investors who want African growth secured against real, tangible assets.
